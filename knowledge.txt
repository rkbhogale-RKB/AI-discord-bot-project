thier are various structures in the game :
Missile Site
This structure allows you to purchase missiles.
Missiles are used to eliminate enemy players and destroy enemy structures.
total max 126 missile can be stored and max cost is around $240,000

SAM Site
Here, you can purchase interceptor.
Interceptor help defend your base by shooting down incoming enemy missiles.
total max 126 interceptor can be stored and max cost is around $240,000

Sentry Guns
Sentry guns provide short-range defense.
They automatically target and attack enemy missiles, aircraft within a 1.5 km radius. it not attack ground unit

Warehouse
Warehouse connects to your resource inventory, enabling you to Transfer or receive resources remotely.
It has a 300-meter operating range for resource transfers.
You can also purchase trucks here, which can collect airdrops and gather resources from longer distances.

Airbase
The airbase allows you to build and store aircraft for combat and support operations.
airbases can be upgraded with 15,000 contruction supplies

Artillery Batteries
This structure stores and fires artillery shells.
It can be set to automatically target enemies, making it highly effective in short-range combat to target tank and infantry.
Artillery batteries have a firing range of up to 30 km.
it can hold chemical shells or normal 155mm shells

Armory
Armory lets you purchase battle tanks and self-propelled anti-aircraft guns.
You can also store ground units here for deployment or defense.

Bunker
Bunker provides protection from radiation and missile attacks.
To stay protected, you must be within a 300-meter radius of the bunker.

Extractor 
This structure allows you to extract resources from nearby deposits.

Radar Station
Radar stations scan the area for aircraft, ships, submarines, infantry, tanks, and structures.
Each radar covers a radius of 800 km.
aircraft based AWACS has only 400km scan radius.
